Install

  1. Open SnazzyPro.dmg.
  2. Drag SnazzyPro into Applications. Updating from 0.2.0 or earlier? Delete the old Snazzy Pro (with a space) from Applications.
  3. Open it, and agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy on the welcome screen.
  4. Pick an AI: Apple's on-device model (Apple Silicon with Apple Intelligence), Ollama on your Mac, or Claude with your own key. No AI? Make decks yourself in the slide editor.

Screen preview asks for Screen Recording permission even though it's switched on? macOS is holding it for an older copy. In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, select Snazzy Pro, click −, then reopen Snazzy Pro and allow it again.

New in 0.2

  • Find any slide: search titles, text and speaker notes across all your decks (⇧⌘F), then jump straight to it.
  • My Decks: decks by name, grouped by category, with tags to filter.
  • Get Decks: add decks from Snazzy Pro's collection or any public GitHub repo.
  • iPhone remote: play and pause the Camera Prompter, and steadier controls.
  • Voice Mode: talk hands-free; the agent runs slides and recording, answers out loud and mutes your mic while it speaks.
  • Chat text size in Settings (⌘+ and ⌘−).
  • Speaker notes on every slide of the sample decks.
  • Camera Prompter: read your notes or a script right under the camera, never recorded.
  • New Layout: re-make a recording with a different layout (camera size, position, screen only…) without recording again.
  • Slide editor: make and edit decks by hand, no AI needed.
  • Live room: smooth for viewers on a VPN or far away, with a new Remote quality for slow links.
  • Lip sync: line up each camera with your mic, with a clap to calibrate.
  • Read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy right on the welcome screen.
  • Vertical clips: turn parts of a recording into 9:16 clips for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
