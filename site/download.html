Install

  1. Open SnazzyPro.dmg.
  2. Drag SnazzyPro into Applications. Updating from 0.2.0 or earlier? Delete the old Snazzy Pro (with a space) from Applications.
  3. Open it, and agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy on the welcome screen.
  4. Pick an AI: Apple's on-device model (Apple Silicon with Apple Intelligence), Ollama on your Mac, or Claude with your own key. No AI? Make decks yourself in the slide editor.

New in 0.2

  • New Layout: re-make a recording with a different layout (camera size, position, screen only…) without recording again.
  • Slide editor: make and edit decks by hand, no AI needed.
  • Live room: smooth for viewers on a VPN or far away, with a new Remote quality for slow links.
  • Lip sync: line up each camera with your mic, with a clap to calibrate.
