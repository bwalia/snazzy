FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs running macOS 15 or later, with Apple Silicon or Intel: one download works on both. Apple Silicon is recommended, especially for local AI models; Apple's on-device model needs Apple Silicon, so Intel Macs use Ollama or a cloud AI.

How do I install it?

Download the DMG, open it and drag Snazzy Pro into Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally. The first time, it asks you to agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. The iPhone and iPad app arrives with the App Store launch.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Run a free local model with Ollama, or use Claude from Anthropic with your own API key. You can mix them: a local model for quick commands, Claude for writing.

Can I use my iPhone or iPad as the camera?

Yes. Connect it by cable and point it at yourself, or use Continuity Camera wirelessly. Snazzy Pro crops out the Camera app's buttons and remembers the framing for each device. The Snazzy Pro app for iPhone and iPad (now in testing) is a remote and teleprompter; recording its own camera in full quality comes next.

Do students need the Snazzy Pro app to take part?

No. Only the presenter needs Snazzy Pro. Students scan the QR code (or open the link and type the room code) in any browser on the same Wi-Fi, on a phone, tablet or laptop. No download, no account, no sign-up. How a live lesson works.

Does the live classroom need Zoom, Teams or the internet?

No. The live room runs directly from your Mac to your students' browsers over your local network. If students are remote, you can stream to YouTube, Twitch or Vimeo instead, or use Zoom or Teams as you do today.

Can I stream to YouTube, LinkedIn or Twitch?

Yes, to one or several at once. Paste each stream key once (they're kept in your Mac's Keychain) and click Go Live. Snazzy Pro asks before every stream, records a copy to your Mac, and lowers the quality automatically if your upload can't keep up. Viewers see you about 2–5 seconds late. Step-by-step guide.

Does my code leave my Mac?

Not with a local model. With a cloud model, Snazzy Pro shows you the exact text (a diff, a transcript or your terminal's text, with likely secrets hidden) and only sends it if you approve. Audio and video are never sent. Share links upload only to storage you own, after you confirm.

Is there a Windows version?

Not at the moment. Snazzy Pro is a native Mac app built on Apple's capture and AI frameworks.

What do recordings look like?

A 1080p video (H.264 with AAC audio) that opens anywhere, plus the raw screen, camera and microphone tracks in a folder next to it, so the camera layout can be changed later.
