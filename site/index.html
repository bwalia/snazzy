Camera

Your camera, framed perfectly.

Use your Mac's camera, a USB webcam, Continuity Camera, or the iPad or iPhone on your desk, connected by cable. Drag and pinch the live preview to frame yourself. Previews never appear in the recording.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs running macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon is recommended, especially for local AI models.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Run a free local model with Ollama, or use Claude from Anthropic with your own API key. You can mix them: a local model for quick commands, Claude for writing.

Can I use my iPhone or iPad as the camera?

Yes. Connect it by cable and point it at yourself, or use Continuity Camera wirelessly. Snazzy Pro crops out the Camera app's buttons and remembers the framing for each device. A dedicated iPhone and iPad companion app is planned.

Does my code leave my Mac?

Not with a local model. With a cloud model, Snazzy Pro shows you the exact text (a diff, a transcript or your terminal's text, with likely secrets hidden) and only sends it if you approve. Audio and video are never sent. Share links upload only to storage you own, after you confirm.

Is there a Windows version?

Not at the moment. Snazzy Pro is a native Mac app built on Apple's capture and AI frameworks.

What do recordings look like?

A 1080p video (H.264 with AAC audio) that opens anywhere, plus the raw screen, camera and microphone tracks in a folder next to it, so the camera layout can be changed later.
