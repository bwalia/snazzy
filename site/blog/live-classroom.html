A live lesson where students only need a browser

8 October 2026

Teach live to everyone in the room, on their own screens, and collect their ideas as you go. Only you need Snazzy Pro. Your students just need a browser.

Do students need the Snazzy Pro app? No. They scan a QR code (or open a link and type a six-letter room code) in Safari, Chrome or Edge on any phone, tablet or laptop on the same Wi-Fi. No download, no account, no sign-up.

How it works

  1. In Snazzy Pro, open the Live tab, type a brainstorm topic and click Start Live Room. It shows you exactly what will be shared and asks first.
  2. A QR code and room code appear. Put them on the board or in your first slide.
  3. Students scan and join. They see your slides or screen with your camera, and hear you (one tap for sound).
  4. They post ideas and vote. You see everything live and can hide or remove anything.
  5. Post a message to everyone, like “two minutes left to vote”, or add your own ideas. You can also ask the assistant: “tell everyone we're moving on”.
  6. Click Turn Ideas into a Deck. The assistant groups the ideas into themes and builds slides with speaker notes, ready for your next lesson.

Why it's different from a video call

  • No accounts or installs for students, and nothing to schedule.
  • Nothing leaves your Wi-Fi. It goes directly from your Mac to their browsers. There are no servers in between, ours or anyone else's.
  • Built for brainstorming: one board, votes, and an assistant that turns the result into slides.
  • The same picture as your recording, so you can record the lesson at the same time.

Good to know

  • Works best for up to 30–50 people on one Wi-Fi network.
  • Students elsewhere can't join a live room. For remote students, stream to YouTube or use the video-call tool you already have.
  • The board is kept only while the room is open. Students' names are whatever they type, and are optional.
  • Run it from across the room with the Snazzy Pro iPhone and iPad remote: change slides and read your speaker notes as a teleprompter.
