See Snazzy Pro working.

Every clip is recorded from the real apps doing the real thing: the Mac app, the iPhone and iPad remote, and a student's browser. No mock-ups. The AI clips use a local model running on the Mac, sped up a little so you don't wait for it.

The AI agent at work

Mac · Voice Mode · with sound

Voice Mode runs a recorded sales talk

“Start recording”, then talk to the audience (ignored), “Snazzy, next slide”, a question about the slide answered out loud with the mic muted, and “Snazzy, stop recording”. Real time, local model.

Mac · Agent · Live room

The agent runs a live lesson

From the chat: open a live room (you approve it on screen), welcome the class, seed the board, close voting, then make a new deck from the top ideas. Real time, local model.

On the Mac

Mac · AI on your Mac

Make a deck by talking

Type (or say) what you need. The assistant plans the slides, writes them with speaker notes, checks its own work and shows you the result. Here with a local model on the Mac: nothing leaves it.

Mac · Live room

Run a live classroom

Start a room, students join from a QR code and post ideas. Votes come in, you send a message to everyone, and the assistant turns the ideas into a deck.

Mac · Slides

Present and record

Speaker notes in front of you, slides in the video. A countdown, then every slide change becomes a chapter in the recording.

Mac · Slides

Start from a sample

Sample decks for twelve sectors (healthcare training, onboarding, sales and more), ready to edit or present.

Mac · Live streaming

Go live online

Choose where to stream, and the same picture as your recording goes out live while you present. Here to a test server; YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitch and Vimeo work the same way.

Mac · Sharing

Share with another Snazzy Pro user

One .snazzy file with the deck (and presets if you like), sent by AirDrop, Messages or Mail. See exactly what's inside first.
