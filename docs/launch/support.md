# Snazzy Pro support

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/bwalia/snazzy/issues. We usually reply within two working days.

## Getting started

1. **Choose an AI model.** Settings › Models. Pick Ollama (on your Mac, private)
   or Anthropic (add your API key in Settings › Providers).
2. **Allow permissions** when asked: camera, microphone, screen recording (then
   quit and reopen), and speech recognition for voice input.
3. **Talk to it:** "Make a 5-minute deck on…", "Use my USB mic and put my iPad
   camera bottom-right", "Start recording".

## Common questions

**My screen preview is black or says permission needed.**
System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording ›
switch on Snazzy Pro, then quit and reopen the app.

**My iPad or iPhone doesn't appear.**
Connect it with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust. It can take up to 30 seconds
to appear. Keep it unlocked with Auto-Lock off while recording.

**The camera inset froze during a recording.**
The recording carries on. The freeze is noted in the recording's
`timeline.json`, and the full raw tracks are kept next to the video.

**Where are my recordings?**
Movies › Snazzy Pro › Recordings.

**Does my data leave my Mac?**
Only if you choose a cloud model, and only after you agree. See the privacy policy.
